1. Screen recording: attached, recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts on the Home Screen, launches the app, records Hebrew speech, transcribes it on the device, and shows the keep-recording choice, the transcript (tap a paragraph to play it, edit mode) and the saved transcripts list. The app has no accounts or login, no user-generated content shared with other users, and no paid content, so there are no such flows to show. Importing a file, sharing a WhatsApp voice note and the Lock Screen controls are described in item 3.

2. Purpose and audience: Ozen (אוזן) transcribes spoken Hebrew into text, entirely on the iPhone. It is for Hebrew speakers who want to turn meetings, lectures, interviews, ideas or WhatsApp voice notes into text. Existing transcription services upload recordings to servers and are weak in Hebrew. Ozen runs its own compact Hebrew speech-recognition model on the device, so recordings never leave the phone, it works offline (including airplane mode), and it needs no account or subscription.

3. How to use it (no login or sample files needed):
- Record: tap the orange ear button, speak Hebrew (or play any Hebrew video/podcast near the phone), tap it again to stop. The app transcribes and opens the transcript, split into timestamped paragraphs. You can then keep or delete the audio.
- Import: tap the upload icon (top left), then "בחירת קובץ", and pick any audio file (m4a, mp3, wav, opus…).
- WhatsApp: in WhatsApp, long-press a voice note, choose Share (under "More…"), and pick אוזן in the share sheet; then open Ozen and it transcribes the voice note.
- Transcript screen: tap a paragraph to play from there; search (magnifier), edit (pencil), tap the title to rename, copy, or export as .txt/.md via the share button.
- Saved transcripts: the list button (top right): swipe left to delete, or "בחירה" to select several.
- Lock screen: while recording, lock the phone; the Live Activity on the Lock Screen / Dynamic Island shows the timer with pause and stop.

4. External services: none. The app makes no network requests. Speech recognition runs on-device with ONNX Runtime (Microsoft, MIT licence) and our own model (Ozen-v1), which is bundled in the app. No AI services, analytics, authentication, payment or data providers are used.

5. Regional differences: none. The app works identically in all regions. Its interface is in Hebrew and it transcribes Hebrew speech.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material: not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party content. The speech-recognition model is our own (Apache-2.0, https://huggingface.co/itayinbar/Ozen-v1).
